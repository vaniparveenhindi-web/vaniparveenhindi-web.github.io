How to Delete Your Account and Data

Users of Harmonix DAW (published by Vani Digital Tech) can request permanent deletion of their account and associated data in either of the following ways:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Harmonix DAW, sign in to your account, go to the Account Screen, and select the option to permanently delete your account and associated account data.
  • Email Request: Send an email to vaniparveenhindi@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Harmonix DAW" and include your account identifier/email so we can verify and process your request.

What Data Is Deleted and What Data May Be Kept

  • Deleted Data: Upon account deletion, your account profile credentials, authentication records, and account-linked app data associated with your account are permanently deleted or de-identified.
  • Locally Stored Files / Audio: Audio projects, recordings, or files stored locally on your own device remain on your device unless you delete them directly or uninstall the app.
  • Data Kept / Retention Period: Certain minimal records (such as financial/billing transaction records or fraud/security/compliance logs) may be retained where required by applicable law, tax/accounting rules, or Google Play billing obligations. Third-party services such as Google AdMob or Google Play services process data according to their own respective policies and retention periods. Deletion requests are typically processed within a reasonable timeframe (generally up to 30 days, unless a longer statutory retention period applies).
For more details on how we handle user privacy, please visit our Harmonix DAW Privacy Policy.
