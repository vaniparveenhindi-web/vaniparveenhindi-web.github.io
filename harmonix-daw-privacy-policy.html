Vani Digital Tech ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information when you use the Harmonix DAW mobile application ("App"). 2. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you use Harmonix DAW, the following categories of data may be collected or processed:

  • Google Account & Authentication Data: When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive basic account information authorized by you, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and unique user identifier used for authentication and account management.
  • In-App Purchase & Billing Information: When you make an in-app purchase through Google Play Billing, payment transactions are processed directly by Google Play. We do not collect or store your full credit/debit card numbers or banking credentials. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, entitlement/receipt verification, and transaction timestamps to unlock purchased features.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob): The App displays advertisements served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction/impression events, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, and improve ads, as well as prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Audio Recordings, Projects & Files: Audio tracks, recordings, MIDI/session data, and imported audio files that you create or load are stored locally on your device (and synced/backed up only if you explicitly enable cloud/account features).
  • Diagnostics, Crash Logs & App Performance: Technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, crash reports, and performance metrics may be collected to maintain low-latency audio stability and fix bugs.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users via Google Sign-In and maintain account access.
  • To verify and restore eligible in-app purchases and unlock paid features.
  • To deliver, cap, measure, and secure advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve audio recording, playback, and DAW performance.
  • To respond to user support inquiries and comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, data may be shared with or processed by third-party service providers that power core app functionality:

  • Cloud / Backend & Diagnostic Providers (if enabled): Authentication, crash reporting, and optional data sync.

You can review Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Security & Retention

Data transmitted between your device and external services is protected using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We retain account and service data only for as long as necessary to provide App functionality, honor purchases, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.
